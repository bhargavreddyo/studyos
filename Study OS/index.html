Frequently Asked Questions

Is StudyOS completely free?

Yes! StudyOS is completely free and will always be free. No subscriptions, no hidden costs, no premium features.

Do I need to create an account?

No account needed! StudyOS works entirely on your device using browser storage. Start using it immediately.

Will my data be lost?

Your data is safely stored in your browser. You can export it anytime and import it later. It won't be lost unless you clear your browser storage.

Does it work offline?

Absolutely! StudyOS works 100% offline. Use it anywhere without internet connection.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes! StudyOS is fully responsive and works perfectly on smartphones, tablets, and desktops.
